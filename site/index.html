Ask companies to delete your personal data

Stealth is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux. It sends data-removal requests to data brokers and other companies that hold your personal details, then keeps track of their replies and follows up when they don't answer.

Download View on GitHub

How it works

  • You choose the companies. Stealth comes with a list of UK data brokers and people-search sites, and you can add others.
  • You approve every letter. Stealth writes each erasure request under UK GDPR (and the equivalent laws in other countries) and sends it only when you say so.
  • It sends from your own email. Letters go from your own email account, so companies' replies come straight back to you.
  • It reads the replies. Stealth spots companies' answers in your inbox, works out whether they've removed you, and reminds them when the legal deadline passes.

Your data stays on your computer

Stealth runs entirely on your own computer. Your details are kept in an encrypted file there, and its key is kept in your computer's own password store. There's no Stealth account and no Stealth server: we never receive your data. See the privacy policy.

Connecting your email

For Gmail, Stealth can sign in with Google. It asks Google for two permissions only: to send the letters you approve, and to read your email so it can spot companies' replies. It can't change or delete anything in your mailbox. Other providers, such as Yahoo and iCloud, connect with an app password.
