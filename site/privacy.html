Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Stealth is a free, open-source desktop app that sends data-removal requests to companies for you. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: Stealth runs on your own computer, and we never receive or store your data.

Stealth runs on your computer

There is no Stealth account, server or cloud service. Everything Stealth knows about you (your name, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, the companies you've contacted, their replies and the evidence Stealth keeps of them) is stored only on your own computer, in an encrypted file. Its encryption key, and your email sign-in, are kept in your computer's own password store (Windows Credential Manager, the macOS Keychain, or the Linux secret service).

The people who make Stealth never receive, see or store any of it. Stealth has no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.

Google user data

If you choose Sign in with Google, Stealth asks Google for two permissions for your Gmail account, and uses them only for these purposes:

  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): to send the removal requests and follow-up letters that you approve, from your own address.
  • View your email messages and settings (gmail.readonly): to find companies' replies to those requests, so Stealth can tell whether they've removed your data and keep a copy as evidence. Stealth also reads your Gmail address, to send from it.

Stealth can't change, label, move or delete anything in your mailbox.

  • Where it goes: Stealth talks to Google directly from your computer. Messages it reads are processed on your computer, and only replies to your requests are kept, in the encrypted file described above. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Sharing: we don't share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. It isn't used for advertising, and it isn't used to develop or train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads it.
  • Keeping and deleting it: your Google sign-in (a "refresh token") stays in your computer's password store until you remove it. You can end Stealth's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and deleting your profile in Stealth deletes everything it kept.

Stealth's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Stealth sends, and to whom

  • Removal requests go only to the companies you choose, and only when you approve them. Each one contains the details you entered that the company needs to find and delete your records, such as your name, address and email address.
  • Your email provider (such as Google, Yahoo or Apple) carries those letters and the replies, as it does for any email you send.
  • GitHub: Stealth downloads its updated list of companies and checks for new versions from this project's page on GitHub. These requests contain no personal data.
  • Company websites: if you ask Stealth to look up a company, it visits that company's website to find its privacy contact. These requests contain no personal data.

Your choices

You can see, change or delete everything Stealth keeps from within the app, or uninstall it and delete its folder. Because we hold no data about you, there's nothing for us to delete, but you're welcome to ask us questions at any time.

Children

Stealth is meant for adults. A parent or guardian can use it on behalf of a child.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Its history is public in the project's code.
