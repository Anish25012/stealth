How it works

  1. Open a site. Stealth opens it in a separate browser window, with none of your own browsing, cookies or passwords.
  2. Search for yourself in that window, as you would anywhere: your name and town.
  3. Come back here and press Capture. Stealth photographs the page that's open, then asks whether it shows you.

People-search sites don't allow programs to search them, so Stealth never searches for you. It opens the site in a window of its own and you search, just as you would in your own browser. When you press Capture, Stealth keeps a screenshot, the page's address and its text, with a fingerprint (SHA-256) so you can show later that it wasn't changed.

Only you can say whether a page shows you: many people share a name. If it does, Stealth writes to that site and puts the page's address in the letter. If they'd already said they removed you, it's a new listing, and a new letter waits for your OK.

After a site removes you, check it again here. A capture that no longer shows you is your proof it's gone.
