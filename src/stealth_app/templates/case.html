Where this stands says what's happening now and what Stealth will do next.

What did they say? appears when there's a reply to read. Stealth reads every reply. When a reply clearly says what happened, and it really is an answer to Stealth's letter, Stealth records it by itself and you'll see "Stealth's reading" in the history. Anything unclear, or any email that only claims to be from the company, waits here for you, with Stealth's best guess already chosen.

If a company asks for ID or more details, Stealth drafts an answer for you to read and send. It asks for nothing a company isn't entitled to.

History is every step, in order. Select an email to read it. The evidence pack is what you'd attach to a complaint to the ICO.
