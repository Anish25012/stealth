What Stealth does

Companies called data brokers collect and sell personal details: names, addresses, phone numbers, who you live with. UK law lets you tell them to delete it. Stealth writes those letters for you, sends them from your own email, keeps every reply, and chases companies that ignore you.

Common questions

Does my information leave my computer?

Only in the letters you approve, which go from your email account to the companies. Stealth has no server and no account, and it sends nothing about you anywhere else.

How long do companies have to answer?

One calendar month from when they get your letter. They can take two months more if they tell you why within that first month. If they ask for ID, the clock pauses until they get it.

A company didn't answer. What now?

Stealth sends them a formal complaint the day after their deadline. If 30 days pass without it being put right, or they refuse, Stealth writes a complaint to the ICO, the UK regulator, for you to send.

Why does Stealth write to the same company again later?

Brokers often re-collect people's details. Stealth asks people-search sites again every 60 days and other companies every 90.

Can Stealth get me removed from everywhere?

No tool can. Some companies ignore the law, data already leaked can't be called back, and new brokers appear. Stealth covers the companies it knows about and keeps the evidence if you need to complain.
