{# What Autopilot does and doesn't do, in one place: the one-time card and Settings both use it. #}
  • First letters to the companies on your list go in daily batches, within your daily limit of {{ settings.daily_send_limit }} emails.
  • Repeat requests go when they're due, because brokers re-list people.
  • Formal complaints go when a company misses its deadline or refuses. Stealth reads your inbox, spam included, first, so a late reply still counts.
  • "Click to confirm" links are opened when the email is really from the company and the link is on their own website. If the page wants a CAPTCHA or a button press, it comes to you.
  • Follow-ups, once per request: if a company asks you to prove who you are without naming documents, Stealth explains politely that it isn't needed. If it asks for more details, Stealth sends the ones your first letter used. If it only says you've been unsubscribed, Stealth asks it to confirm the erasure.

It never sends ID documents. It still asks you before anything to do with ID documents (a passport, licence, bill or photo of ID), web forms, deleting old accounts, replies it can't read with confidence, ICO complaints and letters to optional companies. Everything it sends is listed under Sent automatically, and each company's history says what was sent and why.
