Your credit reports

Each of the three UK credit reference agencies must give you a free statutory credit report. Look for accounts, searches or addresses you don't recognise: they can be the first sign that someone is using your identity. Stealth reminds you to look; it never fetches your reports or sees them.

Mark done when you've finished a step. For steps worth checking again, such as your Google results, Stealth sets a reminder for you. Not for me takes a step out of your score, for example if you don't use social media.

Reminders show here and on the Overview, and Stealth tells you once through the tray icon when one is due. Dismiss a reminder when you've dealt with it.

Credit reports: press I've checked it today after you've looked at an agency's report, and Stealth reminds you when it's time to look again. Statutory reports are free from all three agencies; you don't need a paid subscription.

Every link opens the official website in your web browser. Stealth doesn't fill anything in for you or tell these services anything.
